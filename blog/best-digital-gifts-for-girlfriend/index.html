The best digital gifts for your girlfriend

By the SendLove team · October 12, 2026

Quick answer

A personal letter, digital flowers, a photo album of your relationship, a 'why I love you' list, a date she can accept, or a photo puzzle.

A gift card says "here's something useful." What you probably want to say is "I know you." These digital gifts are built for that.

Heads-up: this site is made by the SendLove team, and SendLove appears in this article. We've tried to be straight about what each option is good at, including the ones that aren't ours.

The one rule

Make it about her, not about the gift. That means using details only you would know: the song she sings off-key in the car, the café where you had your second date, the face she makes when she's pretending not to be impressed. Put those in, and any of the gifts below will feel personal.

8 digital gifts she'll love

1. A letter she'll reread

Not a long one. Three or four paragraphs: a memory, what you love about her, something you've never told her, and something you're looking forward to. She'll come back to it.

2. Flowers on a day she doesn't expect

Sending flowers on a big day is lovely. Sending them after she finally finishes a tough project, or on a grey Monday, shows you were paying attention.

3. Your story as a photo album

First photo to latest, with a caption here and there. Making it is a trip down memory lane for you too.

4. A "why I love you" list

Specific and a bit silly is the sweet spot. "The way you say 'okay okay okay' when you're excited" is the kind of line she'll quote back to you.

5. A date she just has to accept

Plan everything, then let her choose the day and time. You'd be surprised how much it means to not have to organize anything.

6. A puzzle hiding a photo of her

Choose one where she's mid-laugh, not posing. The reveal is the gift.

7. A countdown to your next trip

Booked a weekend away? Send the countdown the same day.

8. A notebook of little things

A few pages of things you've noticed about her that nobody else has. It's the most effort on this list, and it shows.

Where to make them

All eight can be made on SendLove. The Love Letter is free, and the rest (Virtual Flowers, Photo Album, Why I Love You, Plan a Date, Puzzle Gift, Countdown to Reunion and Notebook) are paid gifts. If you only want to send flowers, DigiFlower is a nice dedicated site where you build the bouquet stem by stem. For a scrapbook-style page, GiftFeels is another option.

A few mistakes to avoid

  • Generic quotes. She doesn't want a Pinterest quote. She wants your words, even if they're clumsy.
  • Only big occasions. Small surprises on random days usually mean more.
  • No heads-up. A link out of nowhere can look like spam. Send a short message with it: "Made you something. Open it when you can."
  • The wrong photo. Pick photos she likes of herself. Trust me on this one.
