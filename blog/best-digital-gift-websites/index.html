The best digital gift websites, sorted by what you actually want to send

By the SendLove team · October 12, 2026

Quick answer

It depends on the gift. For personal, interactive gifts for a partner, SendLove has the most variety. For flowers, DigiFlower. For celebrity videos, Cameo. For e-cards, Jacquie Lawson or Punchbowl.

"Digital gift" can mean five different things depending on who you ask. A gift card. An e-card. A video from a celebrity. A personal interactive surprise. So instead of one long ranking, here are the best sites for each kind.

Heads-up: this site is made by the SendLove team, and SendLove appears in this article. We've tried to be straight about what each option is good at, including the ones that aren't ours.

First: what kind of digital gift do you mean?

When people search for a digital gift, they're usually after one of these:

  • A personal gift: something made with your own words and photos, like a letter, album or puzzle.
  • Digital flowers: a bouquet sent as a link.
  • A video message: often from a celebrity or a group of friends.
  • An e-card: a designed greeting card sent by email.
  • A gift card: money to spend at a specific store.

Each one has a "best" site. Mixing them up is how people end up sending a gift card when they wanted to send a feeling.

Best for personal, interactive gifts: SendLove

If you want the person to open something that's clearly about them, this is the category to look at. SendLove focuses on couples and close relationships, with gifts like a love letter (free), photo album, puzzle that reveals a photo, "why I love you" list, reunion countdown, notebook, date plan and a playful "Make Them Say Yes" question.

The main strength is variety. You can send a puzzle this month, a countdown the next, and a letter on your anniversary, without starting over on a new site every time.

Other good options here: AIskyla is fully free with no sign-up (photos and music are removed after 15 days). GiftFeels does occasion pages and digital scrapbooks, with paid add-ons like a custom song. Our GiftVerse makes a 3D room for couples to explore together on a call, as a bigger one-time gift.

Best for digital flowers: DigiFlower

DigiFlower lets you build a bouquet flower by flower, pick a wrap, write a card, and send it as a link. It's free to make, quick, and nicely done. If you want flowers and nothing else, it's a strong choice.

If you'd rather send flowers alongside other personal gifts from one place, SendLove has Virtual Flowers too.

Best for a video message: Cameo

Cameo lets you book a short personalized video from a celebrity, athlete or creator. For a fan, it's a memorable gift. It's also one of the more expensive digital gifts out there, and it's about the celebrity rather than about the two of you, so it fits some occasions better than others.

Best for e-cards: Jacquie Lawson and Punchbowl

Jacquie Lawson is known for beautifully animated e-cards, often with a classic, illustrated look. Punchbowl leans toward invitations and cards for events. Either is a good pick for a greeting to a coworker, a relative or a big list of people.

Best for gift cards: the store itself

Honestly, if you're sending a gift card, buy it straight from the store or app they love. It's the simplest and safest route. A gift card is a great practical gift; it just isn't personal. Some people send a gift card for the practical part and a short personal gift alongside it for the feeling.
