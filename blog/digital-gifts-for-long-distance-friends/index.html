Digital gifts for long-distance friends

By the SendLove team · October 12, 2026

Quick answer

Good digital gifts for a long-distance friend include a photo album of your friendship, a puzzle with your most embarrassing photo together, a thank-you letter for a specific moment, digital flowers on a hard day, a group video from your friends, or a countdown to your next visit.

Friendships don't always survive moves and new cities. The ones that do usually have someone putting in a bit of effort. A small digital gift now and then is an easy way to be that person.

Why friends need gifts too

We put a lot of thought into gifts for partners and almost none into gifts for friends, beyond birthdays. But when your best friend moves away, the little things you used to do (grabbing coffee, dropping by, texting "come over") disappear. A gift for no reason is a way to say "I still think about you, and this still matters."

Digital gift ideas for a faraway friend

A photo album of your friendship

Every bad photo, every night out, every road trip. Caption them with inside jokes. This one tends to get forwarded to the group chat with a lot of crying emojis.

A puzzle with your worst photo together

The more embarrassing, the better. They'll laugh the second they realize what it's going to be.

A thank-you letter

For the time they drove two hours to help you move. For staying on the phone the night everything fell apart. Friends rarely thank each other for the big moments, and it means a lot when someone does.

Flowers on a hard day

A breakup, a lost job, a rough week. Digital flowers with a short "I'm here" message are an easy, kind thing to send when you can't be there in person.

A countdown to your next trip

Planning to visit? Send a countdown. It gives you both something to look forward to.

A group video

Get your old friend group to record a clip each, combine them, and send it on their birthday or after a big life change.

Where to make them

SendLove has a photo album, puzzle, virtual flowers, countdown and a free letter. It's built mostly around couples, so some gift wording is romantic, but the album, puzzle and flowers work just as well between friends. For a group video, any free video app will do.

When to send one

  • Their first week in a new city.
  • After a big win: new job, graduation, an engagement.
  • After a loss or a rough patch.
  • The anniversary of the day you became friends, if you know it.
  • A random weekday, for no reason at all.

Keeping a long-distance friendship alive

Gifts help, but they're not the whole thing. What keeps long-distance friendships going is regular, low-effort contact: a voice note, a meme that made you think of them, a monthly call you both protect. The occasional gift on top of that is what makes them feel special.

Frequently asked questions

What can I send my long-distance best friend?

A photo album of your friendship, a puzzle with a funny photo of you two, a thank-you letter, digital flowers on a hard day, or a group video.

What's a good gift for a friend who moved away?

Something that shows the friendship still matters, like an album of your memories together or a countdown to your next visit.

Can I send a digital gift to a friend?

Yes. Albums, puzzles, flowers and letters work well between friends as well as couples.
