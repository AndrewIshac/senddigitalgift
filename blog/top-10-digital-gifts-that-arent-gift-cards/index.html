Top 10 digital gifts that aren't gift cards

By the SendLove team · October 12, 2026

Quick answer

Digital flowers, a love letter, a photo puzzle, a photo album, a countdown, a date invitation, a notebook, or a group video message.

Gift cards are useful. They're also the gift equivalent of "I didn't know what to get you." If you want something digital that says more than that, start here.

Why skip the gift card?

There's nothing wrong with a gift card. It's practical, and sometimes practical is exactly right. But it's the same code anyone could send. When you want someone to feel thought about, it's worth a few minutes to make something only you could make.

Here are ten ideas, roughly from quickest to most involved.

1. Digital flowers with a real note

Time to make: about 2 minutes.
The easiest upgrade from a text. Pick a bouquet, write two honest lines, send. Works for congratulations, apologies, get-well wishes and "no reason" days.

2. A love letter that opens on screen

About 10 minutes.
Write what doesn't fit in a text: a memory, what they mean to you, something you're looking forward to. It opens like an envelope, which is a nice touch.

3. A "why I love you" list

About 10 minutes.
Easier than a letter because you can write it in fragments. Mix the big reasons with the tiny, weird ones. Those are usually the favourites.

4. A photo puzzle

About 5 minutes.
They solve it to reveal a photo. Pick one with a story behind it: your first trip, a terrible haircut, a dog you both loved.

5. A photo album of your year

15 to 20 minutes.
Go through your camera roll, pick the best 15 or 20, and put them in order. Add a short caption to a few. People love seeing their year through someone else's eyes.

6. A countdown to something

About 3 minutes.
A trip, a reunion, a concert you're going to together. Every time they check it, the number drops. It's a gift that keeps working for days or weeks.

7. A date invitation they can accept

About 5 minutes.
Plan it, then let them pick the day and time right from the gift. Taking the planning off someone's plate is underrated as a romantic gesture.

8. A notebook of shared memories

20 minutes or more.
A few pages of notes, reasons, inside jokes and memories. It's more effort, and it shows.

9. A playful question

About 2 minutes.
"Will you be my valentine?" "Can I take you to dinner Friday?" Make the No button run away when they try to tap it. It's silly, and that's the charm.

10. A video message from friends or family

Varies.
Collect short clips from people who love them and stitch them together. Free to make with any video app, and it can bring people to tears, in a good way.

Where to make them

Gifts 1 to 9 can all be made on SendLove, which has a free love letter and paid gifts like the photo puzzle, album, countdown, date plan, notebook and flowers. If you just want flowers, DigiFlower is a good dedicated option. For number 10, a simple video editor on your phone is enough.

How to pick the right one

  • Short on time? Flowers, a puzzle or a playful question.
  • Want to make them emotional? A letter, a list of reasons or an album.
  • Want to give them something to look forward to? A countdown or a date plan.
  • Want to really go all in? A notebook or a group video.

Digital flowers, a love letter, a photo puzzle, a photo album, a countdown, a date invitation, a notebook, or a group video message.
