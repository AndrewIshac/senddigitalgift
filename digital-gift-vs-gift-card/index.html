Digital gift vs gift card: which should you send?

When people search for a digital gift, they often find gift cards. Both are sent online, but they do very different jobs. Here's how to choose.

What a gift card does well

  • Lets someone pick exactly what they want.
  • Works well for coworkers, clients or people you don't know well.
  • Is practical for big purchases they're saving for.

What a personal digital gift does better

  • Shows you know them, not just their favourite store.
  • Carries your words, photos and shared memories.
  • Creates an emotional moment when they open it.
  • Is something they want to keep and reopen.

Our take

For a partner, a close friend or family, a personal digital gift almost always means more. For colleagues or when you truly have no idea what someone needs, a gift card is a safe choice. You can also do both: a gift card for the practical part, and a personal digital gift for the feeling.

Frequently asked questions

Is a digital gift the same as a gift card?

No. A gift card is store credit. A personal digital gift is an interactive surprise made for one person, with your own message and photos.

Which is more thoughtful?

For people close to you, a personal digital gift is usually more thoughtful because it's about your relationship.
