Digital gift card or personal digital gift?

Both are digital gifts, but they say different things. Here's how to choose.

Digital gift card

  • Gives them money to spend at a store
  • Good when you don't know what they want
  • Same code anyone could send
  • Says "here's something useful"

Personal digital gift

  • Gives them a moment made just for them
  • Good when you want them to feel thought of
  • Carries your message, photos and inside jokes
  • Says "I was thinking about you"

Digital gift FAQ

What is a digital gift?

A digital gift is anything you give online instead of physically. Besides gift cards, it can be a personal, interactive surprise like an animated letter, flowers or a photo album that the person opens from a link.

How is a personal digital gift different from a gift card?

A gift card gives someone money to spend. A personal digital gift gives them a moment made for them, with your own words, photos and little interactions.

How fast does a digital gift arrive?

Instantly. As soon as you share the link, the person can open it, wherever they are.

Can I send a digital gift by text or WhatsApp?

Yes. Each gift is a link, so you can send it by text, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger or email.
