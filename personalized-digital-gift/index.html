How to make a personalized digital gift

Personalization is what turns something digital into something they'll remember. Here's how to make a digital gift that could only be for them.

1. Start with them, not the gift

Before choosing anything, think about the person. What do they love? What have you lived together? What are they going through right now? The answers tell you which gift fits.

2. Choose a format that matches

  • Words-first person? A letter or a notebook.
  • Nostalgic? A photo album.
  • Playful? A photo puzzle or a playful question.
  • Planner? A date invitation.
  • Short on time or just thinking of them? Flowers with a note.

3. Add details only you know

  • Nicknames and inside jokes.
  • The place you met, a song you share, a food you both love.
  • Photos that tell a story, not only perfect ones.

4. Write like you speak

Read your message out loud. If it doesn't sound like you, rewrite it. Personal beats polished.

5. Send it at the right time

When they wake up, before something stressful, or during a call so you can see their reaction. Timing is part of the personalization.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a digital gift personalized?

Your own message, your shared photos and specific memories only you two have.

Can I add photos to a digital gift?

Yes. Albums, puzzles and letters can include your own photos.
