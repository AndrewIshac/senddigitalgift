Digital gifts for her

Whether she's your girlfriend, your wife or your partner, these digital gifts for her are about what you know and love about her, delivered in a way she can open right away.

Ideas she'll keep coming back to

  • A love letter: the things you think but rarely say out loud.
  • Digital flowers: perfect on an ordinary Tuesday, when she least expects it.
  • A memory album: your favourite photos of her and of you two.
  • A date invitation: plan the date and let her accept from the gift.
  • A notebook of little things: what you notice about her that no one else does.
  • A photo puzzle: a playful moment that ends with a picture she loves.

Make it feel like you

She doesn't want a perfect gift; she wants one that sounds like you. Use the way you normally talk, the nickname you use, and memories from your real life together.

Perfect moments to send one

  • When she's travelling for work.
  • After a stressful day.
  • Before something important she's nervous about.
  • On the date you met.
