Digital birthday gifts that arrive right on time

Missed the mail deadline? Living in another city? A digital birthday gift arrives exactly when you want it to, and it can say a lot more than a birthday text.

Why choose a digital birthday gift

  • It's always on time. Make it today, send it today.
  • It works anywhere. No shipping, no address needed.
  • It's personal. Your words and photos, not a store's.
  • It's memorable. Something to open stands out in a feed of birthday messages.

Digital birthday gift ideas

  • Their year in photos: an album of the best moments since their last birthday.
  • A birthday letter: what they mean to you and what you wish for them.
  • Birthday flowers: a digital bouquet with a short, warm message.
  • A birthday puzzle: a fun game that reveals a throwback photo.
  • A celebration plan: invite them to a birthday dinner or outing they can accept right away.

Group birthday idea

Coordinate with friends or family so everyone sends their own digital gift throughout the day. It turns a normal birthday into a day of small surprises.

What to write

Skip the generic "have a great day." Mention one memory from this year, one thing you admire about them, and one thing you'd love to do with them next year.

Frequently asked questions

Can I send a digital birthday gift at midnight?

Yes. Since it's a link, you can send it at exactly midnight so it's the first thing they see.

Is a digital birthday gift okay for a parent or grandparent?

Yes. A family photo album or a heartfelt letter is often a favourite for parents and grandparents, especially if they live far away.
